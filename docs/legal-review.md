## Required owner/legal decisions before launch

1. Confirm the precise operator identity and any required business address, contact, grievance or other disclosures. Do not assume MSME registration establishes a particular entity form.
2. Review applicable Indian privacy, consumer and information-technology obligations, including which DPDP provisions and rules are in force on the actual launch date. Determine any notice, consent, rights-request and grievance requirements. Do not treat this copy as a compliance certification.
3. Confirm hosting/CDN/security providers, log fields, purposes, locations, access and retention. Review any cross-border processing and provider terms.
4. Adopt an operational retention/deletion policy for email enquiries, backups and service correspondence. Current public wording describes proportional handling, not a verified deletion schedule. Confirm the business can implement it.
5. Define a practical identity-verification and information-request process and applicable response requirements.
6. Verify Zoho service configuration and privacy terms. Future analytics/cookies require a new review and any required visitor choices before introduction.
7. Any future form backend needs server-side validation, spam/rate protections, processor review, retention, security and revised notice. Present implementation has no delivery guarantee because the visitor sends email independently.
8. Prepare separate technology engagement agreements covering scope, fees, deliverables, IP, dependencies, responsibilities, timelines and support; none are invented in website terms.
9. Review yoga participation/suitability communications and any appropriate consent/acknowledgement process. Website information is not diagnosis or treatment. Do not collect health histories through the generic enquiry form.
10. Specifically review liability wording and enforceability. Current text preserves non-excludable rights; it deliberately does not add blanket exclusions, arbitrary caps, indemnities, jurisdiction or arbitration clauses.
11. Verify ownership/licences of original text, Living Signal artwork, fonts, open-source software and all temporary imagery. Existing AI/editorial human studies are not practitioner/client portraits and use the owner-authorised editorial treatment; final visual/rights approval is still required. Authentic photography is a future replacement as documented in assets.md, not a prerequisite invented by this implementation.
12. If payments are added, review pricing, cancellation/refund, taxes and consumer disclosures before release. No payment/refund policy has been manufactured here.
13. Confirm revision date and published wording after review. Public pages contain no developer placeholders. Recheck actual deployed cookie/network behaviour; source inspection is not a guarantee about a future host.
