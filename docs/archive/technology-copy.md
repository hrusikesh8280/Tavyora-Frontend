# Technology copy and composition

## Headline alternatives considered

1. Bring the problem, not a perfect brief.
2. A clearer path from problem to product.
3. Start with the problem. Shape what comes next.
4. Software begins with understanding.
5. Make the next technical decision clearer.

Selected **Bring the problem, not a perfect brief.** It lowers the barrier to an enquiry and connects directly to the four visitor situations. The alternatives are narrower or less distinctive; no unsubstantiated superiority claim is needed.

## Narrative decisions

The page starts with the customer's situation, then shows how Tavyora reasons about it. Capabilities follow that context. The journey explains possible responsibilities from understanding through operation without implying that every engagement needs seven stages. The fit section includes an explicit limit: specialist or wider-team needs are established before scope is agreed.

Three engagement starts are specific enough to act on: Build from zero, Improve what exists, Solve one hard problem. Ongoing support is omitted because intent and terms are not confirmed. Prices, timelines, clients, results and availability are not invented.

Technical terms live in capability lists and are paired with plain-language problem descriptions. AI is scoped to workflows, evaluation, error handling and human review.

## Conversion

“Start a technology conversation” opens a prepared email to admin@tavyora.com. “See how we work” leads to the approach chapter. Each problem links to its actual capability row. Breadcrumb, wordmark and footer return to `/`. Wellbeing points to the existing homepage section, not an unbuilt page.

The homepage keeps its existing navigation and CTA destinations because the brief restricts homepage edits to the signal. Access `/technology` directly for this phase's review; no new homepage content or link treatment has been inserted.

## Art direction

No technology photography or placeholder project imagery. All route and journey graphics are local SVG/CSS in the locked semantic palette. The new social image is deterministic typography/vector art, not a fake interface. Existing illustrative AI-generated homepage wellbeing imagery remains visibly labelled and awaits authentic practitioner photography.
