# Production homepage copy decisions

## Locked proposition

**Useful systems. Thoughtful practice.**

The approved headline stays. Supporting copy explicitly states what Tavyora does: digital products, software and intelligent workflows alongside practitioner-led online yoga. No unreleased app is presented as a product people can already use.

The current activity strip has only the three owner-confirmed activities: technology consultancy, software and product development, practitioner-led online yoga.

## Technology

Keep “Bring the problem. We’ll find the starting point.” and the question “What are you trying to solve?” The four situations are Build, Rework, Intelligence and Systems. Capabilities and process labels remain visible semantic HTML; routing is an additional explanation, not the only information.

Refine “one operating system” to “one connected system” in Systems copy so it does not imply an operating-system product. Describe practical work and trade-offs, not unsupported scale or quality claims. AI copy includes evaluation and human review rather than promising autonomous accuracy.

## Wellbeing

Keep “Room to move. Time to notice.” and the owner's qualification statement without inventing a name, institution, biography or certification.

Offerings:

- **One-to-one online yoga:** Individual guided sessions shaped around you, your experience level and your practical goals.
- **Small-group online yoga:** Guided online sessions with clear instruction, considered pacing and space to practise together.

The original microcopy “Practice creates room to notice. Progress has its own pace.” reinforces attentive practice without a therapeutic promise. No course catalogue, dates, pricing, corporate offering, testimonials or health outcomes are implied.

## How Tavyora works

**Direct conversations. Considered work.**

Three practical principles: start with the real problem; keep the thinking connected; make the next step clear. The section describes an independent operating approach, shared design/engineering decisions and understandable scope/handover. It does not claim a large team, years in business or a client record.

## Conversion and navigation

- Hero: Explore technology → `#technology`; Explore wellbeing → `#wellbeing`.
- Technology enquiry links → `admin@tavyora.com` with a technology subject.
- Yoga session enquiry → the same address with a yoga subject.
- Closing: Build something with Tavyora → email; Explore yoga → `#wellbeing`.
- How we work → `#how-we-work`.

These are complete working paths for the homepage-only stage. Switch to substantive internal pages when those are actually built. No dead `/contact`, `/technology` or `/wellbeing` links are introduced.

## Imagery and launch dependencies

The still-life image is illustrative, not the practitioner or an actual venue. Its visible caption is “ILLUSTRATIVE IMAGE / AI-GENERATED”. Final practitioner photographs and their usage permission are owner inputs for a later phase. See `imagery.md` for the detailed art direction.

The approved seven-page first release still needs its remaining pages, actual privacy/service terms and final release checks before publication. None of those is represented as completed by this homepage.
