# Wellbeing content decisions

## Hero directions explored

1. Move with attention. — concise, but does not name the online offering.
2. Make room for practice. — welcoming, but less distinctive.
3. A practice, at your pace. — personal, but could imply that every group session is individually paced.
4. Clear guidance. A little room. — grounded, but fragments the proposition.
5. Online yoga, with room to notice. — selected. Names the offering, avoids an outcome promise and supports a spacious two-part composition in the existing sans/italic family.

## Original editorial lines explored

1. Less to prove. More to notice. **Used in The Practice.**
2. A little room. A place to begin. **Used beside the illustrative image.**
3. Leave room for what you notice. **Used as the quiet editorial pause.**
4. Attention gives movement its place.
5. A practice can begin quietly.
6. Let the pace make room for you.
7. Start with the person, not the pose.
8. Small returns make a practice.
9. Movement deserves a moment of attention.
10. Enough space to begin again.

Only three standalone editorial lines are used. Session-stage captions explain the interaction; they are not borrowed quotations. No famous teacher quotations or spiritual slogans.

## Narrative and factual boundaries

Nine semantic chapters: hero, practice, individual sessions, small-group sessions, illustrated rhythm, practitioner, pause, suitability, enquiry. The composition alternates text, a single image, interaction and empty space instead of repeating cards.

Only current one-to-one and small-group online yoga are offered. There are no age-specific groups, corporate sessions, schedules, packages, prices, courses, retreats or medical outcomes. Group size is not quantified. The session rhythm is explicitly illustrative, not an identical protocol for every session.

The practitioner section uses only the owner's supplied facts: professionally qualified, master's degree in yoga. No name, institution, years of experience, portrait or other credential is invented.

The calm suitability note is limited to considering personal circumstances and seeking appropriate professional guidance where needed. No diagnosis, treatment or therapeutic advice is supplied.
