# Wellbeing SEO

Title: **One-to-One & Small-Group Online Yoga — Tavyora**

Description: Practitioner-led online yoga with clear instruction and considered pacing. Explore one-to-one and small-group sessions, the approach and how to enquire.

Canonical: https://tavyora.com/wellbeing

A current search-language check found “online yoga”, “one-to-one” and “small-group” used directly for these formats. No volume, ranking or traffic estimate was inferred. The title was chosen to describe the two actual offerings precisely, rather than the much broader wellbeing category or unsupported health outcomes. Google recommends concise, descriptive titles; no keyword list is inserted into headings.

Research references:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable
- https://www.amanmerican.com/online-yoga (format-language observation only; no copy, claims, prices or schedules borrowed)

Implementation:

- Static App Router page with one descriptive H1 and nine semantic sections.
- Complete essential HTML without JavaScript; graphics are decorative enhancement.
- Explicit index/follow, unique canonical/title/description and Open Graph/Twitter image metadata.
- Factual Organization, Service and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD. No Person, credentials, events, offers, courses, ratings or reviews in structured data.
- Sitemap now lists exactly `/`, `/technology`, `/wellbeing`. Prototype routes remain noindex and absent from sitemap.
- All three production navigation systems use crawlable real hrefs. Enquiries use email until a real /contact page exists.
- Image disclosure, accurate alt text, reserved dimensions and responsive local sources.
- No thin SEO child pages, analytics, Search Console configuration or deployment.
