## Tokens

- Page: #f8f8f2; ink: #29382c; muted text: #56614f; rule: #bdc3b2; highlighted surface: #e6ebdc; action: #40553a.

- Main reading text 15–16px, compact navigation/actions 12–14px, subordinate annotations 9–11px. Layout is checked for zoom/reflow; annotations carry no unique critical instruction.
- Rectangular surfaces; no general card-grid system, shadows or pill language.
- Fluid grid: 1328px content maximum; margins reduce from 56px desktop to 20px narrow mobile.
- Transitions normally 200ms; signal is scroll-position-driven with no autonomous loop.

## Components

The route is a Server Component. Navigation and the full capability/service text render in HTML. Client islands are only the problem selection, motion preference shell and SVG transformation.

The problem list retains all four situation descriptions. Selection reveals an additional possible path, while the complete capability index below remains visible. All business-critical information exists before click or animation.

Signal geometry interpolates from precise superellipse contours to open organic contours, with line density fading. It is decorative, never a data visualisation. Offscreen and hidden-tab checks prevent ongoing redraw. Mobile processes fewer paths and disables pointer response.

The signature transition has a reduced-motion pair with both endpoints visible. No forced scroll, sticky animation runway, WebGL, video, audio or motion library.
