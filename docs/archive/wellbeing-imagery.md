## Authentic photography brief

Use the actual practitioner with consent. Natural window light; early morning or late afternoon softness; neutral breathable clothing; a real, minimal environment; matte materials; honest skin texture and quiet movement. Include moments between poses. Avoid exaggerated retouching, stock-fitness staging, spa costumes and composited scenery.

Capture:

1. A wide horizontal frame with negative space for potential future hero use.
2. Standing practice.
3. Seated practice.
4. A close detail of hands, posture, mat or fabric.
5. A simple portrait.
6. A landscape-oriented frame with generous negative space.
7. An intentionally composed vertical mobile frame.

Before replacement, confirm rights/consent and which image represents the actual practitioner. Replace the still life with an honest practice image, create responsive WebP/AVIF sources, keep declared dimensions, adjust the existing 40% desktop / 48% mobile crop by visual review, and replace the illustrative caption/alt text with an accurate description. Do not silently turn generated imagery into an apparent practitioner portrait.

The current practitioner section is text-led rather than a fabricated portrait. Real identity and photography remain a release content dependency.
